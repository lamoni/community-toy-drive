How it works

Simple, private, and local, from wish list to Christmas morning.

  1. Add to the list

    A parent, grandparent, or friend of the family fills out a short form with each child's age, sizes and wish list.

  2. We say hello

    A friendly volunteer reaches out to the parent or guardian if we need clarification on any of the suggested gifts or hobbies for a child.

  3. Santa's helpers get to work

    Neighbors pick out gifts or drop off toys. Volunteers sort, match and wrap a gift for each child.

  4. Gifts are delivered

    Gifts are delivered or picked up before Christmas, ready to go under the tree.

Questions

Who can be on Santa's list?

Kids ages 0–17 living in {{serviceArea}}. Just add them to the list.

Can I add my own kids?

Of course! Just choose "My own kids" on the Santa's list form.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. What you share is only seen by the small group of {{organization.name}} volunteers coordinating gifts, and is never shared publicly or sold.

What kinds of toys should I donate?

New, unwrapped toys for any age. Gifts for teens and babies are always the hardest to find, so those are especially appreciated. Please no toy weapons.
